Rules & dictionary

This game follows the classic English 15×15 word-board format: 100 tiles, seven-tile racks, standard letter values, premium squares, 50-point bonus for using all seven rack tiles, exchanges only while at least seven tiles remain in the bag, and standard rack-value endgame deductions.

Dictionary adjudication: this page does not contain the Collins word list. When a play is challenged, use Collins’ official online Scrabble checker. In this build an unsuccessful challenge adds 5 points per challenged word to the player whose move was challenged; a successful challenge removes the move and the challenged player loses that turn.

Casual-use note: the game state is stored in your Firebase Realtime Database. It is designed for private games among friends, not cheat-resistant tournament play.
